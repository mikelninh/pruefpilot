How it works

Evidence first. Authority last.

The model can help with extraction, retrieval and drafting. Deterministic checks and human review remain the boundary around consequential decisions.

1 · IntakePDFs → fingerprints → typed fields
2 · Rules + retrievalversioned sources, effective dates, citations
3 · Evidence checksconfirmed / partial / missing + contradictions
4 · Human reviewbounded next action, no autonomous decision
Boundaries

What this demo does not pretend.

It is a working engineering prototype, not a production government decision system. Real deployment would require stronger identity, access controls, persistence, observability, retention/deletion, security review and qualified domain validation.

Grounded

Strong claims should point to the supporting document or rule.

Bounded

The system prepares work; it does not grant itself authority.

Observable

Fallbacks, uncertainty and quarantined content remain visible.

Human-approved

Funding, benefit, procurement or legal decisions remain human decisions.
