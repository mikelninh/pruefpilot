What it does

Turns a fragmented review into one repeatable workflow.

Instead of a person manually opening files, comparing fields, looking up rules and drafting the next request, the system prepares one structured review state.

1 · IntakeRead PDFs and create document fingerprints.
2 · StructureExtract typed facts from unstructured files.
3 · CheckCompare evidence against versioned rules and surface contradictions.
4 · ReviewPrepare a bounded next action; keep the final decision human.
ProblemManual document reconciliation
InputPDFs + changing rules
OutputEvidence state + visible gaps
OutcomeReviewable next action
Why a team would care

Useful when the data is messy before it is structured.

This is the workflow pattern I would bring into business automation: start from the real documents and rules people already use, then make the repeated review easier to operate and debug.

Speed
Fewer manual comparison steps

The system gathers the relevant facts and gaps into one review state instead of forcing a person to reconcile everything from scratch.

Clarity
Uncertainty stays visible

Missing evidence and contradictions are first-class states, not silently converted into confident prose.

Control
Human decision remains last

The system prepares work but does not grant itself authority over funding, benefits, procurement or legal decisions.

Technical depth

Underneath the simple review: typed intake, retrieval and explicit boundaries.

The frontend is only the visible layer. The project includes a Python/FastAPI backend, typed data contracts, real PDF intake, versioned rules, retrieval, source-aware evidence states, prompt-injection handling and automated engineering tests.

Typed intakeDocuments become fingerprints and structured fields instead of loose prompt context.
Versioned rulesRetrieval keeps effective dates and sources visible rather than treating rules as timeless text.
Prompt-injection quarantineSuspicious document instructions are treated as document content, not trusted system commands.
Evidence statesConfirmed, partial, missing and contradictory evidence remain distinguishable to the reviewer.
Human approval boundaryThe system can prepare a next action; consequential decisions remain outside autonomous execution.
Prototype boundaryThis is an engineering proof, not a production government decision system; identity, tenant isolation, persistence and qualified domain validation would need further hardening.
