      What it does

      Read. Check. Prepare.

      Instead of opening every file, comparing requirements by hand and writing the same follow-up again, PrüfPilot prepares the review so a person can focus on the decision.

      1. Read

      Pull the important facts out of the documents and keep the source attached.

      2. Check

      Compare the file with what should be there and surface gaps or contradictions.

      3. Prepare

      Turn the open points into one clear next step for the reviewer.

      Before → after

      From document hunting to a review-ready case.

      The value is not a fancy AI answer. It is reducing the repetitive work between receiving documents and making a human decision.
