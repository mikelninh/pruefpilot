Why this is useful

It prepares the decision instead of making someone hunt through files.

The system does not approve the grant. It does the repetitive preparation: compare requirements, check whether evidence exists, compare important facts, and turn gaps into one clear follow-up.

Input

Program requirements plus the applicant’s document package.

Check

What is missing? Do important amounts, dates and facts agree?

Output

A review-ready finding and the exact next request for the applicant.

Human remains responsible for approval.

PrüfPilot prepares evidence and open questions so the reviewer can decide faster and with a clearer trail.
