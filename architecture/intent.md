# Intent

## One sentence

Turn documents, versioned rules and evidence gaps into a clear, inspectable next action for a public-sector reviewer while keeping formal decision authority outside the model.

## Primary user

Human reviewers handling document-heavy, rule-bound public-sector cases.

## Problem

Reviewers must reconstruct evidence, apply the correct rule version, spot inconsistencies and request missing material across heterogeneous documents. AI can accelerate extraction/retrieval, but a plausible answer is dangerous when evidence is missing, a rule is outdated or untrusted document content attempts to steer the model.

## Desired outcome

A reviewer can see what is confirmed, partial or missing; inspect the applicable rule/evidence; understand inconsistencies; and approve a prepared next action without surrendering formal authority.

## Non-goals

- autonomous funding, benefit, procurement or legal decisions;
- claiming engineering readiness equals authority acceptance by a public body;
- hiding missing evidence behind confidence scores;
- treating an uploaded document as trusted instructions;
- assuming one domain pack proves all administrative domains.

## Success looks like

The reviewer spends less time reconstructing a case while the system remains fail-closed on missing evidence, tenant boundaries, production readiness and human approval.
