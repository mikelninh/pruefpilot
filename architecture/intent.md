# PrüfPilot — Intent

## Problem
Document-heavy public workflows force reviewers to reconcile PDFs, requirements, versioned rules, missing evidence and inconsistent facts manually. Generic AI can make this faster while also making silent assumptions dangerously easy.

## Outcome
Turn documents, rules and evidence gaps into a **reviewable next action** while preserving uncertainty and final human authority.

## Users
Public-sector and regulated-workflow reviewers handling structured decisions from messy documents.

## North star
Reduce review friction **without lowering evidence quality, traceability or authority discipline**.

## Non-goals
- autonomous legal/funding/benefit decisions;
- treating model extraction as authoritative fact;
- claiming target-agency production acceptance from engineering tests.
